Introduction

Welcome to ChessMentor. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles your information when you use the app.

Because ChessMentor is designed to run entirely offline, we collect minimal data and keep everything on your local device.

Information We Collect

Unlike many modern applications, ChessMentor does not require you to create an account, register, or log in. We do not gather or store any personally identifiable information (PII) on external servers.

  • Local Progress Data: Your chess ratings, puzzle solving history, completed lessons, and settings are saved locally on your device using standard secure local storage.
  • Crash & Performance logs: Apple may collect anonymous diagnostic and usage data if you have consented to share it in your iOS settings. This data helps us improve the stability and performance of the app.

How We Use Your Data

Your local data is used solely to provide and personalize your experience within ChessMentor, including:

  • Tracking your daily streak and progress through lessons.
  • Calculating and adjusting your chess rating based on solved puzzles.
  • Saving your preferences, such as board theme, coordinates, and coaching notifications.

Data Sharing and Security

Since your information is stored exclusively on your device, we do not share, sell, or rent your data to any third parties. Your data remains fully under your control. You can delete all your progress data at any time from the app's settings via the "Reset all progress" option.

Children's Privacy

Our app is fully suitable for children and does not collect any personal data from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date above.
